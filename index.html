Financial Disclaimers & Transparent ROI Methodology

Important Notice: Estimated annual returns displayed on Credit Scout are probabilistic statistical models and do not constitute guaranteed returns. All peer-to-peer micro-lending investments carry risk of capital loss and borrower default. Credit Scout does not provide registered investment advisory services.

1. Expected Net ROI Formula: Net ROI (%) = (Repayment Probability × Interest Rate) - ((1 - Repayment Probability) × LGD)

Where LGD (Loss Given Default) is modeled conservatively at 35% to reflect partial amortized repayments collected prior to default and recovery proceedings.

2. Projected Profit on Investor Capital: Projected Profit (₹) = Investment Amount (₹) × (Expected Net ROI / 100)
3. Multi-Factor Match Score: Match Score = 100 × (w₁ · Safety + w₂ · Credit Score + w₃ · Risk Alignment + w₄ · Yield)

Weights dynamically rebalance based on your chosen risk profile (Low, Medium, or High).
